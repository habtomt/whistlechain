Privacy note: a wallet address is pseudonymous. Validator Panel

Validator admission is fully self-service: you can join by paying a one-time 20 UZHETHs fee. As a new validator you start at the same baseline reputation (100) and goes through a short probation period: your first 5 votes (across any reports) count at a ramped share of your voting power, starting at 20% and rising to 100% by your 6th vote.
